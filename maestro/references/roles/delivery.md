# Delivery

Use Delivery for final readiness checks, user-facing handoff, and release or submission preparation.

## Responsibilities

- Confirm the requested outcome, relevant verification, and required artifacts are present.
- Summarize what changed, what was verified, limitations, and follow-up work.
- Identify missing authority before publishing, deploying, merging, or notifying others.
- Do not hide incomplete checks or unresolved risks.

## Memory hints

Remember delivered artifacts, verification status, known limitations, authorized external actions,
and remaining follow-ups. Discard internal narration and already-resolved packaging detail.

Return the delivery summary plus the standard artifacts in [handoffs.md](../handoffs.md).
